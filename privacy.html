VARM Privacy Policy

VARM is the WhatsApp assistant of the Baramulla Postal Division, India Post. This page explains what information VARM collects and how it is used.

What we collect

When you message VARM, we receive your WhatsApp mobile number, your WhatsApp profile name, and the messages you send. If you take a token, we record the service you chose and the time.

How we use it

We use this information to issue token numbers, answer your questions about India Post services, and, only if you choose "Get scheme updates", to send you information about India Post schemes. You can stop updates anytime by replying STOP.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Your questions may be processed by an AI service to generate answers. We do not share your number or name with any outside party for marketing.

Contact

For questions or to request deletion of your information, contact the Baramulla Postal Division or email baramullapo@gmail.com.
